Northern Ireland passenger rail stations were checked on 22 October 2026
against Translink's Network Statement 2026, Appendices 2 and 3:
https://www.translink.co.uk/getmedia/75f785a5-032e-4327-b11b-bbf683fe5e28/Network_Statement_2025.pdf

Use current passenger-facing names from the network map: York Street
(listed under its former name Yorkgate in the appendix table), Larne Town,
and Belfast Grand Central. Bangor is qualified as Northern Ireland to
distinguish it from Bangor (Gwynedd). No National Rail CRS codes are invented.

`../lib/trainStations.js` merges these 54 stations with the bundled National
Rail directory for header train search. Reporting search retains its existing
directory. Train times open on National Rail using CRS codes; Northern Ireland
selections open Translink's planner, where the station must be selected again.
